$\begin{aligned} \text{解} & \forall x>0,\exists n\geq0使n\leq x\leq n+1 \\ & \text{又 }f(x)\geq0(x\geq0) \\ & 故\int_{0}^{n}f(t)dt\leq\int_{0}^{x}f(t)dt\leq\int_{0}^{n+1}f(t)dt \\ & 故\frac{\int_{0}^{n}f(t)dt}{n+1}\leq\frac{\int_{0}^{x}f(t)dt}{x}\leq\frac{\int_{0}^{n+1}f(t)dt}{n} \\ & x\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{\int_{0}^{n}fotdt}{n+1}=\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{\int_{0}^{n}t-[t]dt}{n+1}=\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{\frac{1}{2}n}{n+1}=\frac{1}{2} \\ & 同理右例极限=\frac{1}{2},由夹逼准创造\lim_{x\to+\infty}\frac{\int_{0}^{x}f(x)dt}{x}=\frac{1}{2} \end{aligned}$
